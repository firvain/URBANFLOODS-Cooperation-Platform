## 1. Who we are and what this platform is

The URBANFLOODS Cooperation Platform (the "Platform"), available at
https://cooperation.urbanfloods.app, is operated by the **University of Western Macedonia
(UOWM)**, Active Urban Planning Zone (ZEP) I, 50100 Kozani, Greece, as Lead Partner of the project
**URBANFLOODS – Promoting Urban Resilience Through an Integrated Advanced Decision Support System
for Flood Control** (IPA-ADRION00374), co-funded by the European Union through the Interreg IPA
ADRION programme 2021–2027.

The Platform is the project's cooperation and knowledge-exchange tool (deliverable D.5.1.1). It
offers a blog, a discussion forum, a members directory and access to the URBANFLOODS KNOWBASE, a
knowledge base of flood-management measures, policies and legislation in the Adriatic-Ionian
region. The Platform is provided free of charge in the public interest.

Contact: Prof. Malamati Louta, Scientific Coordinator, louta@uowm.gr.

## 3. Reading without an account

Everything published on the Platform can be read without an account, in line with the FAIR
principles (findable, accessible, interoperable, reusable) that the project committed to. An
account is needed only to post, reply, comment, report content, or appear in the members
directory.

## 4. Your account

- Registration requires a valid email address, which we verify. Give accurate information and keep
  it up to date. One person, one account. Accounts are personal and may not be shared or
  transferred.
- You are responsible for everything done through your account and for keeping your password
  confidential. Tell us at once if you believe your account has been compromised.
- Project partners and associated partners may be given an **admin** role to moderate content and
  manage members. The Lead Partner holds the **superadmin** role.
- You may close your account at any time from your account settings (section 13).

## 5. How publishing works

- Replies and comments by verified members are published immediately.
- A member's **first** blog post or forum thread is held for review by an admin before it becomes
  public. Once an admin approves it, the member is marked as trusted and later posts and threads
  are published immediately. Admins may remove the trusted status if the rules below are broken.
- Admins may edit titles and formatting for clarity, move content to a more suitable category,
  hide or remove content that breaches these terms, and lock discussions.
- Any member can **report** content. Content that receives several independent reports is hidden
  automatically pending review. Reporting in bad faith is itself a breach of these terms.

## 6. Rules for content and conduct

1. is unlawful, defamatory, harassing, threatening, discriminatory or hateful, or that incites
   violence;
2. infringes the intellectual property, privacy or other rights of any person, including copying
   text, images or data you do not have the right to share under the licence in section 8;
3. contains personal data of third parties without their consent, or **sensitive personal data**
   of any person (health, ethnic origin, political opinions, religion, and similar categories).
   The project does not collect or process such data and the Platform is not the place for it;
4. is commercial advertising, spam, or unrelated to flood management, climate adaptation, civil
   protection and the themes of the project;
5. contains malware, attempts to circumvent security or rate limits, scrapes the Platform
   automatically beyond what the open-data exports provide, or impersonates another person or
   organisation.

Discussions should be respectful and professional. Disagreement is welcome; abuse is not. The
working language of the Platform is English, but content in the other project languages is
welcome and should be marked with its language.

Accounts that are never verified are deleted automatically after 30 days.
